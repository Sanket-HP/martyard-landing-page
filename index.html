The Customer Path

How Martyard Connects Your Shop

Our straightforward pipeline enables brick-and-mortar stores to launch digital storefront catalogues and receive neighborhood dispatches easily.

Step 1

1. Business registers

Step 2

2. Gets verified

Step 3
